# Ways and means
Status: planned
Updated: 2026-09-06

## Purpose
A universal methods database for sales, marketing, and consulting across markets and industries. Japan market entry is one application of this library.

## Scope
Customer discovery, segmentation, prospect qualification, positioning, outreach, proposals, market assessment, consulting delivery, and follow-up.

## First result
One repeatable market-assessment method with inputs, steps, outputs, and decision criteria.
