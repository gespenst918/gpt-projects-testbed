# Japan-market price settings
Maintain pricing references for services and market activity in Japan. This is a research library, not an approved quotation list.

For each entry record:
- Offering, scope, exclusions, and customer segment.
- Amount, currency, unit (hour, day, project, subscription), and tax treatment.
- Geography, effective date, and source/access date.
- Whether it is a published price, historical quote, estimate, or proposed internal setting.
- Assumptions, confidence, and date for review.

Use the [pricing reference template](../../../templates/pricing-reference.md). Keep general pricing methods in [ways and means](../../../projects/ways-and-means/README.md). Do not add identifiable confidential client quotes.

No current prices are asserted by this starter structure.
