# Leiomano vision
Status: working draft based on the agreed repository direction.
Updated: 2026-09-06

## Direction
Build a reusable body of consulting methods, industry knowledge, and practical tools that supports Leiomano's work with businesses developing markets and implementing projects.

## Starting focus
- Consulting for companies exploring or entering Japan.
- Manufacturing, factory automation, and robotics advisory.
- Sales and marketing methods that can apply across markets.
- Internal tools that improve follow-up and repeatable delivery.

## What success looks like
Each project produces something reusable: a method, template, documented case, research finding, or working tool. Evidence and client feedback should guide which services expand.

## Still to decide
Priority customer segments, measurable business targets, and the sequence of future service launches. Exploratory ideas are not commitments.
